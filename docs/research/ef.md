# Case study: Entrepreneurs First (EF)

Research date 2026-11-14. Web research only. [UNCERTAIN] = sources conflict or only a secondary/unverified source. Several primary pages (Sifted, Business Wire) returned 403, so some stats come via search snippets.

## 1. Model and mechanics

**Thesis.** EF backs "individuals who we think will be great founders before they have a company", agnostic on ideas at admission ("we're not going to take the idea into account") ([Matt Clifford, Will Jarvis podcast](https://willjarvis.substack.com/p/104-matt-clifford-ambition-agency)). Founded 2011 by Matt Clifford and Alice Bentinck; Bentinck CEO since Dec 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurs_First)).

**Cohort mechanics (London, as documented by participants, 2023-24).**
- Pre-start access (Slack/Airtable ~5 weeks before), kick-off weekend, then Form: 8 weeks to find a cofounder and a "stable team"; hard stop at week 8, ~50% of cohort leaves unpaired (author's account); cohort 70-80 people, roughly half technical / half commercial; investment committee around week 12; "c. 30-35% of remaining teams get funded"; then Launch (seed raise, £1-5m) ([High Leverage survival guide](https://highleverage.substack.com/p/inside-entrepreneur-first-a-survival)). Another participant: 80+ people, matches in weeks 2-3, reshuffles week 4, "many breakups" week 7 ([Not Sure If Obvious](https://notsureifobvious.beehiiv.com/p/insights-from-doing-entrepreneur)).
- Dan Macdonald's account: deliberate "Halloween Massacre" of teams, no solo founders, exit if no team by deadline ([Part 3](https://danmacd.substack.com/p/is-entrepreneur-first-worth-it-part-467)).
- Another source claims 50 founders/intake, 5% acceptance, 40% reach IC, ~50% of those funded [UNCERTAIN, secondary, French cohort] ([Tim Frin](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-entrepreneurs-firsts-path)). Wikipedia says 1-3% acceptance from tens of thousands of applicants. EF itself says 17,000 applications in 18 months (2022) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/28/entrepreneur-first-raises-158m-at-a-560m-valuation-adding-stripes-collison-brothers-to-its-list-of-backers)). No official acceptance rate found.

**Cities.** London, Bangalore, San Francisco now ([joinef.com](https://www.joinef.com/)). Closed Hong Kong, Berlin, Singapore, Toronto in spring 2023; SF opened Oct 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurs_First)). Paris/Berlin schemes scrapped because customer development on US hours was hard from Europe ([Sifted via search summary](https://sifted.eu/articles/entrepreneurs-first-us-shift)).

**Terms (changing; cite year).**
- 2023: ~$125K for 8% + $7.5K per participant, ~$140K for a 2-person team ([Not Sure If Obvious](https://notsureifobvious.beehiiv.com/p/insights-from-doing-entrepreneur)). Older UK: £80K cash after fees at IC; headline £120K convertible, ~10% at next round [UNCERTAIN, GrowthMentor aggregator] ([source](https://www.growthmentor.com/startup-accelerators/entrepreneur-first)).
- 2026: up to $250K pre-seed, typically $125K on an 8% post-money SAFE plus optional $125K uncapped MFN SAFE ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurs_First)). EF site now advertises an equity-free grant during ideation, up to $250K once formed, up to $5M follow-on through Series B, $600K+ credits, 9 months SF office, SF Demo Day with 200+ VC partners ([joinef.com](https://www.joinef.com/)).

**EF corporate.** $158M Series C at $560M valuation (June 2022), 500+ companies, $10B+ portfolio value, $680M exits ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/28/entrepreneur-first-raises-158m-at-a-560m-valuation-adding-stripes-collison-brothers-to-its-list-of-backers)). March 2026: $200M at $1.3B, portfolio "over $16bn" per Sifted ([Sifted via search](https://sifted.eu/articles/entrepreneurs-first-unicorn-200m-fundraise)); EF site and Wikipedia now say $24B and 600+ companies. Figures differ by source; treat $16-24B as EF's own claim.

## 2. Sourcing and matching

- Sourcing is people-led. EF says it spends ~8 months a year sourcing and "10,000+ hours" per country [UNCERTAIN, EF-derived marketing copy] ([Innovations of the World](https://innovationsoftheworld.com/entrepreneur-first-the-worlds-best-place-to-find-a-co-founder-over-10000-hours-spent-finding-assessing-and-selecting-canadas-most-exceptional-talent/)). In 2025 EF paused open applications for six months to meet exceptional people early in their careers directly; recent cohorts are "some of our smallest in many years" ([EF](https://www.joinef.com/posts/talent-the-emerging-frontier-of-investing)).
- Signals: "edge" (a specific advantage suited to a type of company); site criteria: consistent outperformance vs peers, prior building, US ambition ([joinef.com](https://www.joinef.com/)). 2022: EF said it used data science on past applicants to find criteria predicting funding success, and "Form" software for matching ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/28/entrepreneur-first-raises-158m-at-a-560m-valuation-adding-stripes-collison-brothers-to-its-list-of-backers)). No public detail on the actual features or tooling.
- Matching: marketplace-style. Founders rank potential partners; 48-hour offsites ([Tim Frin](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-entrepreneurs-firsts-path)). Clifford engineers "no awkwardness" breakups via button-click team registration and public Slack announcements; the average founder goes through ~2.5 partnerships; most teams form in the first few weeks of a 3-month programme; first $1B company took ~10 years ([Jarvis podcast](https://willjarvis.substack.com/p/104-matt-clifford-ambition-agency)).
- Failure rate: ~50% of cohort unpaired at week 8 in one account; funded rate 30-35% of remaining teams. An "80% success rate" pairing claim exists [UNCERTAIN, marketing copy]. No audited figure for what share of funded teams survive 2-3 years.

## 4. One unicorn end to end: Tractable

- **Founders.** Alex Dalyac: French, Imperial College ML grad, ~1 year work experience, discovered deep learning via Hinton's Coursera course in 2013. Razvan Ranca: Cambridge ML researcher who "topped EF's technical assessment". Adrien Cohen (co-founded Lazada, sold to Alibaba) joined after seed as President ([Tractable blog](https://tractable.ai/how-we-built-an-ai-unicorn-in-6-years/), [Teardown/search](https://www.joinef.com/companies/tractable/)).
- **Meeting.** Dalyac and Ranca met at EF London in 2014, in the final hackathon interview where they coded all night ([Tractable blog](https://tractable.ai/how-we-built-an-ai-unicorn-in-6-years/)).
- **Pivot.** First customer was plastic pipe weld inspection (stopped), then car insurance claims ([same](https://tractable.ai/how-we-built-an-ai-unicorn-in-6-years/)).
- **Seed.** ~$1.9M, June 2015. Investors: Zetta Venture Partners (lead per Nordic9), Acequia, EF, Charlie Songhurst ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/funding_round/tractable-seed--d41488e5), [Nordic9](https://nordic9.com/news/tractable-raised-19m-in-a-round-led-by-zetta-ventures/)). Key intro: Matt Clifford introduced Songhurst (ex-Google/Microsoft angel), who started with $50K and pulled in US angels; one angel wrote $300K after a 15-minute call ([Tractable blog](https://tractable.ai/how-we-built-an-ai-unicorn-in-6-years/)). Songhurst is now a backer of EF itself.
- **Series A.** ~$8M, ~2017, Zetta (Ash Fontana) per Tractable; Ignition Partners also cited. Date and lead [UNCERTAIN].
- **Series B.** Led by Insight Partners, July 2018, after "10x YoY growth" and first $1M customer contract. Amount [UNCERTAIN]: one source says $25M ([search result](https://www.insurancetimes.co.uk/news/tractable-raises-25m-with-view-to-expand-ai-proposition-worldwide/1432680.article)); verify.
- **Series C.** $25M, 27 Feb 2020, Georgian lead, Insight and Ignition participating ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/tractable-raises-25m-in-series-c-investment-round-led-by-georgian-partners-to-further-scale-ai-for-accident-and-disaster-recovery-worldwide-301012164.html)).
- **Series D.** $60M, June 2021, Insight and Georgian co-led, $1B post-money ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/18/tractable-snaps-up-65m-led-by-softbank-for-car-and-property-damage-appraisals-using-ai)).
- **Series E.** $65M, July 2023, SoftBank Vision Fund 2 lead; total ~$185M ([same](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/18/tractable-snaps-up-65m-led-by-softbank-for-car-and-property-damage-appraisals-using-ai)).
- **What mattered.** (1) EF match produced a technical pair; (2) EF partner intro to one angel unlocked the seed; (3) public customer announcements created competitive pressure across Japanese insurers; (4) a repeat investor chain (Zetta -> Insight -> Georgian -> SoftBank). Timeline: 2014 EF to $1B in ~7 years (Tractable's own "6 years" framing).
- Caveat: Wikipedia's Tractable page does not mention EF; EF involvement rests on Tractable's blog and EF's company page.

## 5. Pain points and gaps

- **Matching is lossy and stressful.** Reshuffles, "existential crisis" week 5, breakups week 7; a participant finished without a cofounder ([Not Sure If Obvious](https://notsureifobvious.beehiiv.com/p/insights-from-doing-entrepreneur)); another dropped after 10 weeks with no validated idea ([Macdonald](https://danmacd.substack.com/p/is-entrepreneur-first-worth-it-part-467)).
- **Incentive misalignment.** "EF is incentivised to create the best possible teams", not individual outcomes; staff encourage breakups; a team was rejected because "EF hated the market" ([High Leverage](https://highleverage.substack.com/p/inside-entrepreneur-first-a-survival)).
- **Weak domain depth.** Technical founders with no sector expertise end in "solution in search of a problem" ([Macdonald](https://danmacd.substack.com/p/is-entrepreneur-first-worth-it-part-467)). EF's own coach: founders over-build before validation ([Tim Frin](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-entrepreneurs-firsts-path)).
- **Follow-on investors.** EF moved Demo Day to SF (2022) to reach tier-1 US VCs; a founder reported 45 investor meetings in four weeks post-Demo Day with no follow-up meeting (search summary of an EF founder account; exact source not verified). EF said 50% of rounds in its Sept 2024 cohort included US funding, mostly US-led (search summary, not verified at source). EF now promises up to $5M follow-on through Series B ([joinef.com](https://www.joinef.com/)), which implicitly admits the gap.
- **Post-programme support** was absent from participant write-ups; no Reddit/HN threads of substance found.

## 6. Where Cala-style graph data plugs in

Cala fields assumed: person career history, company, round (lead, date, amount), investor co-investment, events.

1. **Edge-scout lead list (user: EF/pre-seed talent investor).** Job: find people with outlier trajectories before they apply (e.g. ex-founder of acquired startup, early engineer at a company that raised a Series B in last 24 months, PhD to big-lab path). Needs: person, employer, role start/end, company funding rounds, acquisition events, event attendance.
2. **Cofounder complement finder (user: solo technical founder / EF cohort manager).** Job: suggest cofounders whose past companies, sectors and roles complement the founder, ranked by prior shared-network overlap. Needs: career history, sector tags, role type, past-company exit/outcome, shared-employer edges.
3. **Follow-on investor mapper (user: EF founder at Demo Day).** Job: after pre-seed, rank investors by fit: who led pre-seed/seed rounds in the same sector and stage in the last 18 months, who co-invests with the founder's existing backers (warm path). Needs: round lead/participants, date, amount, stage, sector, investor co-investor edges.
4. **Unicorn path benchmark (user: investor/founder).** Job: for a given company show the seed -> A -> B chain of comparable companies (like Tractable: Zetta -> Insight -> Georgian -> SoftBank) and typical gap times. Needs: ordered rounds, leads, dates, amounts, valuation where known.
5. **Event-to-intro (user: founder at Slush-type event).** Job: who attending has led rounds in my sector, and who in my network can introduce. Needs: event attendee list, person-investor links, past-company edges.

Pitch angle: sourcing (1), matching (2) and follow-on (3) map to the three gaps above. Idea 3 has the clearest, most verifiable pain (post-Demo Day silence).
