# Demo pitch script (about 60 seconds, biotech, fictional company)

> Fictional founder and company. Lines starting with `#` or `>` are not spoken. Read at a natural pace, about 2.5 words a second.
> Trigger order is deliberate: London, therapeutics, antibody, pre-seed, raise, machine learning, traction, team.
> `scripts/build-replay.ts` turns this into `demo-pitch.json`. Edit the words, then run `npm run build-replay`.

Hi, I'm Amara, founder of Helixa Bio, a London startup. We're building therapeutics for autoimmune disease, specifically AI-designed antibody drugs. Today, drug discovery means years of trial and error in the lab before you find one good antibody. Patients wait while that happens, and most current treatments only manage symptoms. We use machine learning to design candidates in weeks, then test them in our own wet lab. We're pre-seed, and we're raising £1.2 million. So far we have a signed letter of intent from a large pharma company, and three academic labs are already using our designs. Our first lab validation showed the antibody binds the target as predicted. Our team is two PhDs: I did my doctorate in immunology at Imperial, and my co-founder has a PhD in machine learning from UCL. This round takes us to our first animal study. We'd love to meet investors who back early therapeutics companies.
